WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

How can I join Velokhaya?
Young people who are interested in cycling and committed to their education can apply to join Velokhaya. Entry is based on interest, commitment, and readiness to engage with both training and learning.
Do you accept bike donations?
We accept bicycles, cycling equipment, and components that meet our programme needs and safety standards. Please contact us to arrange a drop off.
How can I support the academy financially?
Individuals and organisations can support Velokhaya through monthly contributions, programme support, or strategic partnerships. Visit our Support page for more details.
Where is Velokhaya located?
We are based at 40 Makabeni Road, Village 1 North, Khayelitsha, Cape Town, 7784.
